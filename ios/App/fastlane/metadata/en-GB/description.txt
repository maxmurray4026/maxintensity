Two warm-ups, then the two sets that matter: a heavy working set and a back-off straight after. Tempo 3-1-3-1. Add 2.5% when you clear the reps. Upper, lower, upper, lower, so every muscle gets a growth signal three times a week.

• A rank from your lifts, and a heavier bar every time you climb one

• Meals: one big meal a day, a quick breakfast, snacks — log by photo, voice or a tap, and plan your week around your shop and budget
• A coach that knows Max's method and your induction, and rebuilds any session in plain language
• Streaks, XP, a league with the people you train with, and a wall for real results

The program, the rank, the calendar and the projection are free. The coach, session rebuilds, meal prep and photo assessment are part of the membership, with a free trial to start.
