Max Intensity · Privacy Policy · Last updated 30 September 2026

Your data stays yours.

Max Intensity ("the app", "we") is a strength-training program and coach, available on the web at maxintensity.app and as an iOS app. This policy explains what we collect, what we don't, and why.

What stays on your device

  • Your training data — program, sets, weights, logs, rank, calendar, bodyweight and nutrition targets — is stored on your phone (or in your browser). We do not upload it to our servers.
  • Photos you take for the before/after and weekly check-ins are stored on your device only and are never uploaded, unless you explicitly send one to the coach for an assessment (see below).
  • Backups you export are files you control.

What is sent to us

  • Coach messages. When you use Max AI (coach chat, session rebuilds, meal prep, photo assessment) the text, and any photo you choose to send, plus the context needed to answer (your plan, rank, priorities and induction answers) are sent to our server and processed by an AI model provider to generate the reply. We use them only to produce that reply and to keep the service reliable; we do not sell them or use them for advertising.
  • Community wall. If you choose to put your name up, the handle, rank, level and streak you post are public to other members.
  • Push notifications. If you turn alerts on, your device's push token is stored so we can send the notifications you asked for. Turn alerts off in Settings to remove it.
  • Account. If you sign in with Apple or email, we store the identifier Apple gives us (and your email if you share it) to recognise you across devices.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We receive a signed transaction record (product and status) to unlock membership; we never see your payment details.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad tracking, no selling of data.
  • No third-party analytics SDKs in the iOS app.
  • No access to your contacts, location or health data (Health integration, if added later, will ask for permission first and be read-only).

Retention and deletion

On-device data is deleted when you delete the app or clear it in Settings. Server-side data (coach message logs, push tokens, account identifiers, wall posts) is kept only as long as needed to run the service. To delete everything we hold about you, email maxmurray4026@gmail.com from the address on your account and we'll confirm within 30 days.

Children

Max Intensity is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under UK and EU GDPR) you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict processing. Contact us at the address above.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we'll update the date at the top and note it in the app.
