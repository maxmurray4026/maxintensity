# Anatomy plates — credits and sourcing

The app's "vintage anatomy layer" places 19th-century-style anatomical
engravings behind stat cards, graphs, progress bars and section headers at
12–25% opacity, tinted bone/red on the black, with the grain on top.

## What ships in this folder

| File | Used behind | Subject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `skeleton.svg` | Rank, progress, leaderboard, the funnel's rank reveal and projection | Skeleton, anterior |
| `legs.svg` | Lower-body sessions (Legs 1, Legs 2, Glute Focus) | Muscles of the lower limb |
| `back.svg` | Upper-body sessions (Upper 1, Upper 2) | Muscles of the back and shoulder |
| `torso-heart.svg` | Nutrition | Heart and thorax |
| `arm.svg` | Arm-priority sessions, session recap | Muscles of the arm |

These five are **original engraving-style drawings generated by
`make-plates.py`** in this folder (contour line + directional hatching, one
ink, transparent ground). They are released into the public domain (CC0) with
this repository. They exist because the build environment used to assemble
this layer could not reach Wellcome Collection or Wikimedia Commons to
download the historical plates, and the layer had to ship working.

## Swapping in the real plates

The layer is file-based: replace any file above with a real plate of the same
subject and filename and nothing else changes. The app masks the image by its
alpha (SVG) or its luminance (JPG/PNG — set `lum: true` on the plate entry in
`app/anatomy.jsx`), so a scan works best as **dark ink on a white ground,
cropped tight, ~1200px on the long side, JPEG quality 70**.

Public-domain sources to use, all free of copyright:

- **Bourgery, J. M. & Jacob, N. H. — *Traité complet de l'anatomie de
  l'homme* (1831–1854).** Wellcome Collection (Public Domain Mark) and
  Wikimedia Commons (`Category:Traité complet de l'anatomie de l'homme`).
  Best plates: muscles of the thigh (anterior/posterior), muscles of the back,
  the heart in situ.
- **Krause, W. — *Handbuch der menschlichen Anatomie* (1876–1880).**
  Wikimedia Commons. Clean woodcuts of limb musculature.
- **Vesalius, A. — *De humani corporis fabrica* (1543).** Wellcome Collection
  / Wikimedia Commons (`Category:De humani corporis fabrica`). The "muscle
  men" and skeleton plates.
- **Gray, H. — *Anatomy of the Human Body* (20th ed., 1918).** Wikimedia
  Commons (`Category:Gray's Anatomy plates`). Public domain in the US and UK.

Suggested search terms: "Bourgery Jacob muscles thigh", "Vesalius skeleton
anterior", "Bourgery heart", "Gray's Anatomy plate 434 (dorsal muscles)".

When you swap a plate in, add a line here in the form:

```
skeleton.svg — Vesalius, De humani corporis fabrica (1543), Wellcome Collection, Public Domain Mark. Cropped, converted to ink-on-white.
```

the priority picker and session recap) is drawn inline in `app/anatomy.jsx`
and is original work, CC0 with this repository.
